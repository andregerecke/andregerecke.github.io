01 · Leadership

How I lead AI transformation

Tools are the easy part. What makes AI scale is clear direction, people who feel able to use it, and a reliable path from idea to working solution.

Direction

Conceived the OPEA AI Strategy 2026 and designed the 2027 AI Enablement and Scaling Concept, with a roadmap through 2029.

  • A six-phase method linking capability building with use-case delivery
  • Six-to-eight-week AI Value Waves with evidence-based impact measurement

My leadership principles

  1. 01

    Problem first, tool second.

    Every AI initiative starts with a business owner and a process that hurts. If no one would miss the solution, we don’t build it.

  2. 02

    Learn by building.

    Workshops end with a working prototype, not a slide deck. Small, safe experiments beat long concept phases.

  3. 03

    Grow people who don’t need me.

    I measure my leadership by how much my team and champions can do without me. Coaching is how AI scales.

  4. 04

    Measure what changes.

    Days saved, decisions improved, adoption that lasts. Impact is tracked from the start, not reported at the end.

Coaching in practice

From graduate to independent AI builder

I coached a graduate in our team with one clear goal: full independence, not just support.

  1. LearnWe designed AI personas side by side, from the business problem to a tested, working persona.
  2. ApplyStep by step, I handed over ownership: first co-facilitating workshops, then working directly with business teams on their use cases.
  3. OwnToday the graduate independently develops AI personas, co-facilitates workshops and contributes to business-led AI projects.

One person coached well multiplies a team’s reach. This is the model I bring to building an AI transformation team.

02 · Selected work

AI in everyday business

Three use cases built with the business teams who own the process, measured against the time it took before.

Procurement

Marge, the market expert

Challenge
Negotiation preparation needs current market intelligence on macroeconomics and supply-chain risks, which takes a long time to compile by hand.
Approach
An AI persona that researches market developments and supply-chain risks to prepare buyers for negotiations, later extended into a Procurement Support Agent.
€200k–500kbetter negotiation results (reported) · ~30 days saved · 15% less time

Real estate accounting

Contract Validator

Challenge
Validating real-estate contracts and their cover sheets was manual, repetitive and slow.
Approach
An AI persona in DocumentDialogue, the internal document-AI platform, that supports the review of contracts and cover sheets.
~33%less contract-validation time · 43 days saved

Development IT

Typo

Challenge
Turning working notes into consistent documentation meant hours of manual editing.
Approach
An AI persona that turns working material into documentation, applying grammar checks and standard templates.
75%less time · ~29 days saved · consistent documentation

Time savings are estimates reported for each use case.
